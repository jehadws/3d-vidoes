# Quality Standards

Automated QA catches technical problems. **Human visual review decides whether shots look good.** A human approves before final rendering.

## Five gates

1. **Story & shot validation** — every narration segment has a visual idea; no gaps or disconnected shots. *(automated: schema + reference checks)*
2. **Asset validation** — missing assets, inconsistent scale, wrong materials, broken rigs, invalid references. *(automated; `--strict` for finals)*
3. **Composition review** — framing, character visibility, silhouette readability, hierarchy, via contact sheets. *(human)*
4. **Animation review** — acting, transitions, foot sliding, clipping, timing, unnatural motion. *(human)*
5. **Final video review** — render artifacts, flicker, continuity, VO sync, audio levels, captions, encoding. *(human + automated)*

## Scoring (gates 3–5)

Score 1–5 on: story comprehension, silhouette/expression readability, acting and timing, composition, color/lighting consistency, camera, prop interaction/continuity, render integrity. Average ≥ 4, none below 3, any 1 = reject.

## Rejection conditions

Identity or proportion drift; unreadable expression at playback size; props floating/clipping; foot sliding; lighting or palette inconsistency; unmotivated camera motion; background overpowering the subject; flicker or sync errors.

Retain contact sheets and review notes for each approved milestone.
