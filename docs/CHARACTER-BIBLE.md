# Character Bible — Protagonist

**Status:** TEMPLATE. No design is approved. Fill in during Phase 1; the protagonist must be original.

The protagonist is the most important asset in the system. Nothing downstream (rig, animation library, shots) starts until this document is approved.

## Required sections

- **Concept:** role in the stories, personality, what makes the silhouette recognizable.
- **Model sheet:** turnarounds (front, 3/4, side, back) and proportion chart (head-heights, limb ratios).
- **Materials:** skin/cloth/hair material rules, palette references, shading style.
- **Expressions:** neutral, happy, confused, surprised, worried (minimum set), with reference renders.
- **Pose library:** key poses that define the character's body language.
- **Rig spec:** body and face controls, IK/FK, constraints, naming convention, deformation limits.
- **Animation rules:** timing tendencies, posture habits, gestures to use/avoid, off-model examples.
- **Technical:** scale/units, poly budget, texture resolution, file layout under `assets/characters/protagonist/`.
