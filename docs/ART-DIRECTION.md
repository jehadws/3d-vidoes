# Art Direction — Stylized 3D

**Status:** proposal, pending Phase 1 approval (reopened for 3D, see [DECISION-LOG](DECISION-LOG.md))
**Medium:** stylized 3D animation, rendered in Blender
**Priority:** quality and story clarity over production speed

## Originality

All characters, silhouettes, palettes, props, and environments are original. References inform broad principles (readable acting, clear staging) only. Never reproduce another creator's characters, scenes, or assets.

## Pillars

| Pillar | Intent |
|---|---|
| Characters | Distinctive silhouettes, controlled proportions, expressive faces, consistent materials |
| Environments | Detailed but simplified worlds designed for storytelling, not realism |
| Cinematography | Intentional camera movement; close-ups, wides, depth, visual storytelling |
| Lighting | Soft shadows, controlled contrast, atmospheric lighting, consistent grade |

## Rules (establish before building the engine)

1. **Stylized, not childish:** cinematic proportions, mature visual storytelling.
2. **Consistent protagonist:** one recognizable main character in every video.
3. **Expressive animation:** body language, faces, gestures, and reactions matter.
4. **Designed environments:** every location feels part of one visual universe.
5. **Controlled palette:** each scene may have its own mood without looking like a different production.
6. **Intentional composition:** every shot has a clear visual purpose.

## Palette and look

Not approved. Palette, shading style, and linework/outline decisions (if any) must come from look-development renders in Phase 1/2, judged at delivery size. Record outcomes here and in the decision log.

## Decision priorities

1. Story comprehension over visual complexity.
2. Original identity over resemblance to any reference.
3. Strong acting and timing over effects.
4. Consistency over one-off spectacle.
5. Reviewable quality over raw throughput.
