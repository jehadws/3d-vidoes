# Quality Standards — Hybrid 2.5D

**Priority:** visual quality and story clarity  
**Review principle:** technical correctness is necessary, but never sufficient

## 1. Review dimensions

Score each from 1–5:
1. Story comprehension.
2. Character silhouette and expression readability.
3. Pose, timing, and acting.
4. Composition and visual hierarchy.
5. Color, linework, and style consistency.
6. Layering, depth, and camera movement.
7. Prop interaction and continuity.
8. Render and edit integrity.

A shot should average at least 4/5, with no dimension below 3. Any score of 1 is an automatic rejection.

## 2. Visual rejection conditions

Reject a shot for:
- Character identity or proportions drifting.
- Expression or gesture unreadable at normal playback size.
- Flat character layers visibly separating or intersecting incorrectly.
- Props floating, clipping, or failing to contact the character.
- Excessive parallax or camera motion distracting from the story.
- Inconsistent outlines, palette, shadow, or typography.
- Background detail overpowering the subject.
- Unmotivated motion, awkward holds, or robotic timing.
- Text or graphics obscuring faces or important action.

## 3. Technical checks

- All assets resolve and have explicit versions.
- No missing image layers, fonts, textures, or linked files.
- Camera framing and safe areas remain valid.
- Frame rate, resolution, audio sync, and shot durations match project settings.
- No accidental alpha fringes, seams, flicker, or layer popping.
- Final export is reviewed at normal speed and full delivery resolution.

## 4. Review gates

1. **Design review:** character, palette, linework, and environment style.
2. **Animatic review:** timing, shot sequence, visual comprehension.
3. **Blocking review:** poses, prop placement, camera and parallax.
4. **Animation review:** acting, transitions, secondary motion.
5. **Final review:** compositing, audio sync, export, and continuity.

Retain contact sheets, representative frames, and review notes for each approved milestone.
